Just Two Courses: A Decade of Diabetes Reversed - Off Insulin and Drugs

Published: February 3, 2026

BNC autologous stem cell care - breaking free from glucose

Ms. Chen, 58, had lived with diabetes for over a decade - daily insulin and glucose drugs, sugar levels on a rollercoaster. On medication her fasting glucose was 12.72 mmol/L, with ALT at 63 U/L and uric acid at 391.1 umol/L; the abnormal numbers filled her with fear of complications.

Yet after two customized courses (four infusions) of autologous mesenchymal stem cells, she stopped insulin and oral drugs; glucose stabilized in the normal range, and liver function and uric acid returned to normal.
