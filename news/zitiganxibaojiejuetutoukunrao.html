Published: October 2, 2024

Osteoarthritis is the most common joint disease: 355 million patients worldwide, one-third in China - 120 million, one in ten people.

01

What is osteoarthritis?

A chronic disease of cartilage degeneration ending in joint degeneration and bone proliferation.

Simply, pathology of joint aging and wear - pain, tenderness, stiffness, swelling, limited motion and deformity: the disability killer

02

Conventional treatment?

Medication

NSAIDs, intra-articular steroids and cartilage protectants.

Non-drug treatment

Acupuncture and physiotherapy; in TCM it belongs to bi syndrome.

Limited effect; progression continues to replacement - never fully equal to one's own.

(2) Secreted factors inducing chondrogenesis and self-repair
