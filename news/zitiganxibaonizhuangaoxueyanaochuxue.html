40 Hemorrhage Cases Treated: Notable Language and Limb Recovery

Published: May 12, 2025

Worldwide, kidney disease incidence keeps rising, eroding quality of life. China alone has over 120 million chronic kidney disease patients - a major global burden.

In recent years autologous stem cell therapy has shown great potential as an innovation - new possibility for kidney patients.

BNC's core autologous stem cell technology

BNC draws the client's blood, reverses white cells into autologous stem cells and reinfuses them - over 200 million cells per session, autologous and thus safer, efficient, rejection-free and side-effect-free.

The treatment principle

Stem cells - the seed cells - self-renew, differentiate multi-lineage and proliferate strongly. For kidney disease, BNC prepares three autologous types to repair injury:

Autologous mesenchymal stem cells

They can differentiate into renal parenchymal cells - mesangial, epithelial, podocytes, endothelial clusters - and modulate immunity to cut inflammatory factors, easing renal inflammation and protecting against further damage

Autologous hematopoietic stem cells

With long-term self-renewal and differentiation into all blood and immune cells, they migrate to damaged kidney regions and become specific renal cells such as tubular epithelium, directly replenishing tissue.

Autologous kidney stem cells

They home directly to injured kidney areas, becoming mesangial, epithelial, podocyte and endothelial cells - participating directly in repair and nephron regeneration.
