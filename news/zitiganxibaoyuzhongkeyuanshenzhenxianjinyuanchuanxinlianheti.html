Strong Alliance: CAS Shenzhen Institute and BNC Build a Stem Cell Innovation Consortium

Published: April 25, 2025

Introduction: innovating together for life and health

On March 28, 2025, the Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology (CAS) and BNC Life Health Technology announced a joint stem cell innovation consortium

1. Why autologous stem cells: BNC's core trend

As regenerative medicine's frontier, autologous technology uses the patient's own cells - extraction, culture and reinfusion - for tissue repair, anti-aging and disease care

- Zero rejection: autologous, no matching, higher safety;

- Precise repair: activating self-healing, targeting damaged tissue;

- Compliant and controlled: international standards, whole-process quality control.

BNC has applied autologous stem cells in chronic disease repair, immune modulation and anti-aging - over 90% effectiveness, new hope for thousands.

2. The consortium: two-way research-industry empowerment

Drawing on SIAT's research strength and BNC's industrial experience, the consortium focuses on three directions:

- Technical breakthrough: optimizing extraction, expansion and directed differentiation;

- Clinical translation: accelerating therapies for diabetes, Alzheimer's and more;

- Standard-setting: industry normalization and international voice.

The China-Europe Medical Center will integrate European pharma resources, building a Bay Area cell-therapy hub and taking BNC global.

3. Future trend: the golden age of autologous stem cells

Driven by policy and demand, autologous therapy is exploding:

- Policy: the 14th Five-Year bioeconomy plan supports stem cells; Shenzhen's 20+8 policy favors life health;

- Demand: the global market may exceed USD 200 billion by 2025; China's aging drives anti-aging and chronic-care demand;

- Technology: automated culture and gene-editing combinations will break bottlenecks.

We believe autologous stem cells are not just medicine's future but everyone's reachable health guarantee.

Closing: embrace cell technology for a healthy future

The consortium marks another BNC leap in regenerative medicine. Patient-centered, BNC will keep making the technology accessible - letting life science truly serve human health and longevity.
