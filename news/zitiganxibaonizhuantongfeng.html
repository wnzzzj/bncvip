Goodbye Gout: Stem Cells Bring New Hope to 170 Million Patients

Published: May 4, 2025

The 2017 China Gout Status Report shows 170 million people with hyperuricemia and over 80 million with gout - mainly men over 30, trending younger.

The most direct marker is uric acid: sustained levels above 420 umol/L warrant high alert.

Above 420 umol/L blood saturates; at pH 7.4 urate crystals deposit in serum, circulating to harm organs and vessels.

High uric acid often signals gout risk

Beyond this value, crystals deposit wherever they land, causing unbearable pain there - worse, gout is more than pain, inducing diseases that injure kidneys and heart.

Effective gout control is a shared clinical challenge; stem cell technology is bringing new options

Gout: more than just pain

Gout is a common, complex arthritis: hyperuricemia with recurrent acute attacks, tophaceous chronic arthritis and joint deformity.

Long-term hyperuricemia mutates urate-synthesis enzymes, disrupting purine metabolism - overproduction and under-excretion deposit urate in joints and kidneys, forming gout.

Patients carry hyperuricemia, crystals, arthritis and kidney stones - badly affecting quality of life.

Urate crystals gather in joints, causing severe pain

High uric acid plus these symptoms warrants alert:

- Finger, toe or joint pain worsening at night;

- Hypersensitive, hot, swollen joints;

- Red or purple skin over joints;

- Limited joint motion;

- Itching or peeling skin as pain subsides.

High uric acid does not guarantee gout - but long-term elevation plus ignored symptoms and pain-only management let crystals deposit permanently as tophi.

These stones cause swelling and deformity, and over time may break skin, ulcerate, infect, compress nerves and rob patients of normal movement.

Mass urate deposits in tissues and organs induce many diseases:

1. In joints: gouty arthritis and tophi, even disability - over half in the big toe, later feet, ankles, knees, elbows;

2. In kidneys: gouty nephropathy - renal insufficiency, acute failure, uremia;

3. In urinary tract: stones;

4. In vessels: worsened cardiovascular disease, atherosclerosis, stenosis, coronary disease - acute MI risk 26% higher;

5. In pancreas: islet damage, insulin resistance, diabetes - 20-50% of hyperuricemia patients are diabetic.

Gout is thus systemic - harming tissues, joints and multiple organs

Gout is one sign of chronic hyperuricemia; uncontrolled uric acid induces many diseases

Untreated, gout brings chronic kidney issues in 95% (impotence, nephritis, stones), cardiovascular disease in 80%, severe joint deformity in 50%

Gout is lifelong and extremely hard to cure.

Stem cells for gout -

new hope for gout and its complications

Clinical gout care is fairly mature but remains pain- and symptom-centered.

Traditional care - drugs, diet, lifestyle - mainly eases pain and controls uric acid with NSAIDs and urate-lowering drugs.

Beyond gout itself, chronically high uric acid harms joints, tissues and organs.

Kidney deposits bring urate nephropathy, stones and failure risk; vessel irritation brings atherosclerosis, hypertension, heart disease and stroke

Only by controlling uric acid, inflammation and immunity while repairing tissue can symptoms be minimized and progression eased at the root.

Stem cell therapy brings new hope.

Stem cells raise immune function, restore organ metabolism, and repair and regenerate damaged tissue and vessels

1. Suppressing inflammation

Stem cells modulate immunity and fight inflammation; attacks hinge on crystal-driven inflammation causing pain and damage.

2. Tissue repair and regeneration

Gout damages cartilage and synovium; stem cells differentiate and promote repair and regeneration.

3. Inhibiting crystal formation

Crystals are the key pathology; studies suggest stem cells influence urate metabolism and crystallization.

4. Antioxidant action

Antioxidant effects reduce free radicals and oxidative joint injury, slowing progression and curbing relapse.

Joint injury is a main feature, and stem cell efficacy for arthritis is clinically confirmed

MSC therapy improves experimental gouty arthritis, suggesting early COX-2 induction

The study examined Ad-MSC delivery's effect on MSU-injected rabbits' inflammation and its mechanisms.

Systemic Ad-MSC via the right femoral artery eased synovial and systemic inflammation

Data show systemic MSC delivery improves joint inflammation in acute gout models; swelling at peak was markedly reduced, with full resolution at 72 hours

For those with limited conventional options - multimorbid, elderly, renal pathology or NSAID intolerance - MSC delivery may be a therapeutic opportunity.

Gout often injures kidneys, and declining function raises gout frequency - a vicious cycle. Rescuing the gout kidney matters as much as treating gout

After BM-MSC transplant, BUN, Scr and 24h proteinuria fell significantly versus controls

BM-MSCs promoted renal cell differentiation and growth, resisted oxidative stress and reduced TGF-beta1 to inhibit EMT - improving chronic failure.
