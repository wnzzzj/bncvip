9 Changes After Stem Cell Infusion

Published: September 4, 2024

UK scientist Anastasia wrote in Nature: stem cells are vital to self-repair and regeneration; their loss is the main cause of aging.

In short, aging's root: new cells insufficient to replace the old - fewer total cells (shrinking) and falling vitality (aging symptoms); ultimately one's own stem cells

The most scientific approach: slowing aging from within, stopping the acceleration effect - stem cell anti-aging.

Stem cells can slow human aging

In modern anti-aging research, stem cells hold a central place - watched by scientists worldwide, carrying humanity's dream.

CAS academician Zhou Qi: 'The value of stem cells lies in their essence - self-replication, proliferation and differentiation into tissues'

Principles of stem cell anti-aging

01. Tissue repair

Aging damages tissues; IV or local MSC delivery uses homing

02. Metabolic regulation

Longevity hinges on strong metabolism; MSCs strengthen it

03. Immune regulation

MSCs - low immunogenic, immunomodulatory - play a key role in inflammation repair

What changes will stem cells bring?

Rejuvenated brain function

After 25 neurons decline about 0.8% yearly - only 55% remain by 70.

Neural stem cells provide new brain cells - notably memory and cognition in dementia.

Rejuvenated heart function

Cardiac stem cells, guided by SDF-1, integrate into damaged myocardium

Rejuvenated liver function

Liver stem cells support synthesis, detoxification and bilirubin clearance

Rejuvenated lung function

Lung stem cells home to the lung, replacing damaged cells and generating new ones

Regeneration outpacing destructive inflammation, suppressing it

Rejuvenated kidney function

Kidney stem cells home to lesions, improving microcirculation and metabolism

They also form new islet cells, gradually normalizing glucose and protecting the kidneys.

Rejuvenated joints

Joint damage is hard to repair and leads to irreversible degeneration.

Under induction they become bone, cartilage, muscle, tendon, ligament, nerve, liver, cardiac and other cells

Transplants replenish stem cells, promoting local bone repair.
