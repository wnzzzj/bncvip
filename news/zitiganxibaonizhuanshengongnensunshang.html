Early Kidney Damage Is Reversible - Timing Is the Key

Published: April 27, 2025

Kidney disease rises yearly worldwide; China has over 120 million chronic patients. And kidney damage is irreversible!

Recently stem cell therapy, as an innovation, has brought new hope.

The kidneys clear metabolic waste and maintain internal balance.

Kidney disease covers structural and functional change from many causes, presenting as hematuria, proteinuria, edema, hypertension, anemia, lumbar pain and oliguria.

For diseases conventional means cannot crack, how do stem cells fare?

Stem cells' hallmark is regenerative differentiation - by injection or transplant they promote renal repair. Autologous cells come from the patient; allogeneic from donors

They act through several mechanisms:

Promoting regeneration: differentiating into tubular and endothelial cells to replace the damaged;

Immune modulation: tuning responses to ease inflammation and protect tissue;

Improved flow: promoting microcirculation to raise blood and oxygen supply.

Autologous MSCs and their exosomes restrain overactive immunity - blocking pathological immune cells and antibodies such as anti-DNA autoantibodies

Application cases across chronic failure, acute injury, diabetic nephropathy, lupus nephritis and uremia:

Positive improvement in chronic renal failure

Case: Ms. Li, 60, long-term chronic failure - creatinine 600 umol/L with anemia and fatigue. After two infusions creatinine fell to 200

Good recovery in acute kidney injury

Case: Mr. Zhang, 45, acute injury - creatinine 400 with fatigue and nausea. After one infusion it fell rapidly to normal and symptoms vanished

Reversal in diabetic nephropathy

Case: fifteen diabetic nephropathy patients treated - blood pressure, glucose and renal markers all improved.

Hope in lupus nephritis

Case: among twelve lupus nephritis patients, those adding stem cells showed clearly better symptoms and renal markers.

Markedly fewer dialyses in uremia

Case: Mr. Wang, 70, uremia - creatinine 1000 with heart failure. After three infusions creatinine fell to 800 and dialysis dropped sharply

These cases affirm stem cells' positive effects across kidney disease - repairing function, easing symptoms, even cutting dialysis. Not omnipotent: treat early
