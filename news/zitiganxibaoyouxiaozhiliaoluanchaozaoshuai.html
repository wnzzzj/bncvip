An Ovarian Comeback: Targeted Stem Cell Infusion Raised Her AMH from 0.01 to 1.01

Published: February 9, 2026

The comeback: AMH from 0.01 to 1.01

Case 1: Ms. Cao, 43, once trapped in fertility struggles - two failed IVF rounds from few, poor-quality eggs, pelvic effusion causing severe ovarian adhesion, hormones fully out of balance. When conventional treatment reached deadlock, she chose BNC's autologous stem cell care - an ovarian rebirth that stunned observers.

A medical marvel in 45 days

BNC experts combined autologous hematopoietic stem cells (IV infusion) with autologous germline stem cells (targeted infusion). After two courses and more than 200 million cells, the 45-day recheck stunned:

AMH: soared from 0.01 to 1.01 ng/ml (reference 0.2-1.5) - ovarian reserve recovering, a qualitative leap;

FSH: dropped from 25.54 to 8.3 mIU/L - hormones back in normal range;

Pelvic effusion: the 2.19 cm fluid vanished entirely, with clear ovarian structure.

"On day 28 after treatment my period returned naturally," Ms. Cao shared excitedly. "Now we are trying for a baby - I can finally look forward to new life!"

From ovarian decline to natural conception

Case 2: Ms. Yang, 47, with ovarian failure, irregular periods and nine consecutive miscarriages. Hoping for a child she had taken hormones for nearly two years to maintain ovulation - but her period stopped whenever the drugs stopped, trapping her in a dependence-decline cycle. Ultrasound showed only compressed sinusoidal follicles; her ovaries were like withered trees unlikely to recover alone.

She received three courses totaling 200 million cells - autologous mesenchymal, hematopoietic and germline stem cells combined.

Follow-up ultrasounds twice showed mature and dominant follicles, enlarged ovaries, higher antral counts, with estradiol and AMH gradually rising. Most uplifting: after stopping medication she kept a regular cycle, conceived naturally and delivered a healthy baby.

Ultrasound before and after care

BNC customized autologous stem cell repair

In organ-function repair - especially multi-system comprehensive intervention - autologous stem cell therapy shows ever-broader potential. For complex problems like ovarian decline that involve endocrine, reproductive and whole-body state, single means rarely suffice; customized combinations of multiple autologous stem cells are becoming a frontier direction.

For ovarian decline BNC applies frontier technology, preparing multiple stem cell types that repair in synergy for systematic care.

Autologous mesenchymal stem cells: strong paracrine, immune-modulating and regenerative capacity - homing to damaged ovaries and secreting VEGF, HGF, IGF and other factors that promote follicle development and improve the microenvironment.

Autologous germline stem cells: theoretically able to differentiate into oogonia or supporting cells, directly rebuilding reproductive function.

Autologous hematopoietic stem cells: mainly rebuilding blood systems, but their cytokines indirectly support the repair microenvironment and immune stability - the supportive soil for other stem cells.

Combining the three covers structural repair (follicle regeneration), functional recovery (hormone secretion) and systemic support (immune-metabolic balance) - a paradigm shift from local patching to whole-system regulation.

Clinical evidence and practical feasibility

More than 150 clinical studies confirm that autologous stem cell transplants significantly improve serum hormones in premature ovarian failure (E2 up, FSH down), increase ovarian volume, ease menopausal symptoms, and even achieve natural or assisted pregnancy. One Phase II trial showed 7.07% natural pregnancies and 14.14% successful IVF

Animal experiments confirm the potential to promote follicle regeneration

Future prospects of autologous stem cells

The technology brings hope of fertility to premature-ovarian-failure patients and shows huge anti-aging potential. Research suggests autologous stem cells may delay ovarian aging through mechanisms including the PI3K/Akt pathway.

Ovarian failure is not a terminal verdict - it is the body's repair signal. Stem cell therapy is a key of life opening rebirth for perimenopausal women. Whatever the age, as long as a spark of ovarian function remains, stem cells can make the withered tree bloom, turning winter of fertility into a spring of hope.

BNC autologous stem cell care - making ovarian reversal possible, bringing every mother's dream within reach.
