When the Pancreas Meets Its Repair Team: Diabetes Care Enters the Cell Era

Published: March 6, 2026

"Did you test your glucose today?"

"Can't eat this, daren't touch that"

"Never stop the meds, never skip the injection"

For people with diabetes these are daily incantations. As islet beta cells decline and insulin falls short, sugar metabolism runs like a machine out of control - glucose swings, complications shadow every step: blurred vision, numb limbs, wounds that won't heal... each symptom signals the islets going on strike while health drains away.

While conventional treatment still stops at 'external insulin' or 'stimulating secretion', BNC has opened a new chapter of repairing islet function with autologous stem cells: somatic cells from the client's own blood are reverse-differentiated into stem cells - a repair team that both rebuilds the islets and calms the immune system, solving diabetes at its root.

01. The core technology: turning somatic cells into stem cells

BNC's core strength is reverse-differentiating somatic cells into autologous stem cells. Developed over 20 years by Lin Xiongbin's team, small-molecule induction turns blood mononuclear cells into autologous stem cells - including 11 functional types such as autologous islet stem cells.

Versus conventional stem cell technology, BNC's advantages stand out:

Safe with zero rejection: cells come from one's own blood - no immune rejection, no ethical controversy;

Efficient at scale: hundreds of millions of highly active stem cells within 7-12 days, high purity and strong differentiation potential;

Mature: six generations of iteration, 20+ years of clinical use without major adverse events - safety proven by time.

02. A two-pronged defense: repairer plus mediator for the islets

01. The repairer: replenishing the islets and rebuilding secretion

BNC's autologous stem cells precisely locate damaged islets and repair them in two ways:

Directed differentiation: directly becoming fully functional beta cells, restoring islet numbers and the capacity to secrete insulin;

Microenvironment repair: secreting nutritional factors that mend damaged tissue and revive the vitality of surviving beta cells.

This is not external insulin replacement - it is rebuilding the body's own glucose-control system, breaking diabetes's vicious cycle at the root.

02. The mediator: cooling immunity to stop islet damage

For diabetes driven by immune disorder, the cells' immune-modulating power is crucial:

Suppressing overactive immune cells and reducing their attacks on beta cells;

Rebalancing immunity and easing chronic inflammation to create a gentle environment for repair;

Improving insulin resistance in liver and muscle so insulin works more efficiently.

03. Clinical validation: real efficacy over 20+ years

Case 1:

Ms. Chen, 56, with years of diabetes: insulin every three days plus oral drugs; fasting glucose before care was 12.72 mmol/L.

After four courses of BNC stem cell care: insulin and drugs stopped; glucose now steady at 7.0 mmol/L.

Case 2:

Ms. Feng, 70, needed medication four times daily; glucose on medication was 8.68 mmol/L - still high.

After two courses: medication down from four times daily to once; glucose steady at 6.0 mmol/L.

Case 3:

Mr. Lin, 39, ten years of diabetes plus ankylosing spondylitis: chronic high glucose left severe ulcers on hands and feet, glucose at 20 mmol/L, needing insulin plus drugs; AS left his lumbar joints stiff and painful, unable to bend.

After four courses: the ulcers gradually healed with new skin; insulin and drugs stopped, glucose at 8-9 mmol/L; back pain eased and he can bend again.

Case 4:

Mr. Lai, 43, long diabetes history: 10 units of insulin daily plus a special glucose pill every 15 days, yet glucose stayed around 10 mmol/L.

After one course: the special pill stopped and insulin halved from 10 to 5 units; now in his second course, post-meal glucose is 7.2 mmol/L - within normal range.

Over 20+ years BNC has accumulated abundant real cases:

Glucose improvement: most patients see clear drops in fasting and post-meal glucose and HbA1c, with reduced or discontinued medication;

Complication relief: diabetic foot, numbness and other complications improve; ulcers heal and quality of life rises.

04. The future is here: redefining diabetes care with technology

From lifelong control to repair and reversal, BNC's autologous stem cell technology is redrawing the boundaries of diabetes care. As regenerative medicine advances, treatment will no longer mean living with drugs - it will mean awakening the body's own repair.
