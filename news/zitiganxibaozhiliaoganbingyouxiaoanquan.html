Clinical cases for liver disease




1 Cirrhosis

45 decompensated cirrhosis patients: 30 MSCs, 15 saline; at one year ascites fell markedly with improved function。serum albumin rose and bilirubin fell,Child-Pughscores also improved markedly.



2 Alcoholic liver disease

A Phase II trial: markedly improved Child-Pugh scores and lowered TGF-beta1, type I collagen and alpha-SMA.

MSCs reduce fibrosis and improve function.


3Autoimmune liver disease

Studies observed MSC efficacy in autoimmune liver disease.

Six autoimmune hepatitis-cirrhosis patients all improved clinically.Lab markers ALB, ALT and AST improved significantly - active efficacy.



4 Liver failure

A Zhongshan trial: 110 patients, 56 receiving MSCs atop standard therapy.

At 24 weeks survival was 73.2% versus 55.6%, with bilirubin and MELD markedly better - significant efficacy in liver failure.



5 Ascites

MSC treatment markedly reduced cirrhotic ascites.

One patient fell from 46.6 to 6.6 mm at 48 weeks with albumin rising - positive for ascites and function.

Marked efficacy and unrivaled advantages across liver diseases.
