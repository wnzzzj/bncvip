Don't Let Your Parting Line Betray Your Age! CGF Plants Youth Back on Your Scalp

Published: April 16, 2026

Struggling in the vortex of hair loss, anti-shedding shampoos and growth serums bringing little? Try CGF concentrated growth factor hair therapy - the follicle-regeneration code extracted from your own blood - and reclaim your lost density!

What is CGF? Hair-growing gold refined from your own blood

CGF (concentrated growth factors) is a consortium of high-concentration actives separated from your own venous blood by variable-speed centrifugation. Not a foreign chemical but a natural nutrient pack refined from your blood - platelets, leukocytes, fibrin, CD34+ stem cell factors and more - with maximal safety and zero rejection risk.

Versus common PRP, CGF's finer centrifugation retains more scalp-repair components. Where PRP relies mainly on platelet-released growth factors, CGF adds leukocytes and a fibrin scaffold - nourishing follicles while regulating the scalp microenvironment and resisting inflammation, a more complete answer to sensitivity and follicle shrinkage.

CGF - a deep revival for your follicles

Hair growth depends entirely on follicles. As androgens, malnutrition or stress shrink them, hair thins, softens and finally sheds. CGF is an all-round spa for follicles, reactivating their vitality at the root:

Wakes dormant follicles: PDGF, VEGF and other factors stimulate follicle stem-cell proliferation and differentiation - rousing the dormant follicles back into the growth cycle.

Improves scalp microcirculation: promotes new vessels around follicles - like channeling fresh water to parched land - so follicles drink ample oxygen and nutrients and leave malnourishment behind.

Builds a growth scaffold: the three-dimensional fibrin scaffold guides orderly stem-cell alignment and differentiation while steadily releasing actives - a stable home for growth.

Regulates the scalp microenvironment: leukocytes suppress inflammation, easing oiliness, itch and redness - restoring the scalp as fertile soil for hair.

The full treatment process - easing hair loss simply

The CGF process is simple and easy - about one hour in total, with normal life resuming the same day:

Pre-assessment: the physician checks follicle status with trichoscopy, then blood tests, confirming CGF suits you.

Numbing prep: topical anesthetic is applied to the thinning area for about 30 minutes - comfortable even for the pain-shy.

Draw and preparation: 10-20 ml of venous blood - as easy as a routine test - is gradient-centrifuged into CGF in about 15 minutes.

Precise injection: ultra-fine needles deliver CGF from dermis to follicle root in a grid pattern - only slight pricks, entirely tolerable.

Course plan: typically 3-6 sessions a month apart. Most notice reduced shedding after the second or third; new baby hairs appear around three months.

Who CGF follicle therapy suits

- Androgenetic alopecia (receding hairline, crown thinning, widening part - men and women alike);

- Stable alopecia areata or telogen effluvium;

- Temporary postpartum, post-surgery or stress-related shedding seeking faster recovery;

- Hair growing finer, softer and slower with visibly less volume;

- Poor response to or intolerance of medication, seeking a drug-free option.

Of course, a physician consultation beforehand is essential to rule out contraindications and ensure safe, effective treatment.

After-care tips that multiply the results

For 24-48 hours after treatment, keep the area dry; avoid strenuous exercise and saunas;

A few may have mild soreness, redness or bruising that subsides within 1-2 days - no need to worry;

Keep healthy habits: no late nights, less smoking and alcohol, fewer oily and sugary foods, more protein and vitamins;

Hair loss is chronic - one or two consolidating courses yearly, with medication or physical therapy as needed, maintain long-term results.
