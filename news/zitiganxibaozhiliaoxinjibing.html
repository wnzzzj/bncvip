China's Stem Cells Join the World's First Tier: New Hope for Six Diseases in 2026

Published: February 2, 2026

In 2025 the National Health Commission officially listed 'stem cell research and organ repair' among the five key special projects of the national key R&D program - marking a full acceleration from laboratory to clinic. CCTV specially reported that China is speeding stem cells into clinical use, bringing new hope to patients with difficult, severe diseases.

The latest data shows over 140 registered stem cell clinical studies in China, spanning six disease areas including diabetes, osteoarthritis and cirrhosis. As policy dividends release, institutions and companies are accelerating the lab-to-clinic journey.

Policy dividends released: cell therapy's golden era

Its inclusion among the five key projects means unprecedented policy and funding support through the latter half of the 14th Five-Year Plan and 2035 vision - spanning fate regulation, organ-regeneration mechanisms, core technologies, product development and clinical research across the whole chain

Six disease areas: clinical wins keep coming

As research and application deepen, breakthroughs across multiple fields bring new hope to patients with refractory disease.

Diabetes

Shanghai Changzheng Hospital reported a patient with 25 years of diabetes plus end-stage renal disease who, after transplant of islets differentiated from autologous induced pluripotent stem cells, remained insulin-free with stable glucose for over 33 months.

Osteoarthritis

In osteoarthritis, a novel stem cell gel injection shows outstanding results: three months post-treatment, joint mobility recovered nearly 60% and pain scores fell 70% - far better than conventional care.

Cirrhosis

In cirrhosis, results are similarly notable: PLA General Hospital reports 83.9% of patients improved in liver function with reduced fibrosis after stem cell intervention; one alcoholic-cirrhosis patient stayed stable for a year after three infusions.

Kidney injury and diabetic nephropathy

For kidney injury and diabetic nephropathy, Changzheng Hospital research confirms glomerular repair with over 75% effectiveness and average creatinine decline of 40%.

Fatty liver

In fatty liver, too, potential is huge: Hospital 301 data shows 45.5% of patients achieved disease reversal after 20 weeks of stem cell intervention.

Premature ovarian failure

Infertile women treated with stem cells later showed increased follicle counts and successful pregnancies; China has delivered the world's first healthy baby from stem cell composite-scaffold treatment of premature ovarian failure.

These are the fruits of exploration across six fields. With marked success in diabetes, osteoarthritis, cirrhosis, kidney injury, fatty liver and premature ovarian failure, stem cell therapy fully demonstrates regenerative medicine's potential - and deeper research will bring hope to many more patients.

Four advantages that break conventional bottlenecks

Compared with conventional treatment, repair-centered stem cells break through multiple bottlenecks.

(1) Precise targeting: like guided missiles, stem cells reach damaged tissue directly - solving the scattered-force problem of drugs while raising efficacy and lowering side effects.

(2) Rebuilt immune balance: stem cells tune the immune microenvironment, avoiding common immunotherapy side effects - a new idea for autoimmune disease.

(3) Complication prevention: secreted growth factors optimize the local environment, lowering secondary risks of dialysis or surgery and raising quality of life.

(4) Long-lasting: clinical data shows a single stem cell treatment can hold effect for over three years - greatly improving the treatment experience.

Moreover, autologous stem cell infusions avoid rejection - high quality, high purity, high efficacy and many types - fully guaranteeing safety.

Closing words

Driven by the special program, innovation across industry, academia and medicine is accelerating. Policy support plus clinical breakthrough may make stem cell therapy a new pillar against chronic and refractory disease.

As MST officials noted, China has mastered core key technologies in stem cells - moving from follower to peer and even leader. The coming five years will be a golden period for stem cell research and organ repair.
