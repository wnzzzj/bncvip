Guangzhou BNC: Blood-Derived Autologous Stem Cells - Company Introduction

Published: August 15, 2025

The BNC Autologous Stem Cell Technology Research Institute is a national high-tech enterprise with independent innovation and autologous cultivation capability - a professional research institution in life-energy production technology and precision regenerative medicine R&D and translation, specializing in scaled non-transgenic autologous stem cell production technology, preparation, storage and translational research.

On December 8, 2024, the Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology (CAS) and BNC's core company jointly established an innovation consortium to safeguard BNC's world-leading stem cell technology in application.

The nature and role of autologous stem cells

Simply put, our parents' sperm and egg created the zygote, embryo and newborn - our lives. Autologous stem cells are the organ seed cells our parents gifted each of us, letting organs grow and mature; they are even more the life-energy cells of every person

The hereditary nature of autologous stem cells

The cells our parents gave us remain in our bodies for life, in every organ and tissue. As life-energy cells they govern growth, development, maturation, life function and content, sexuality, fertility, reproduction, repair and defense

The consumability of life's performance

These life-energy cells - autologous stem cells - slowly consume and burn themselves as they perform each person's life; when one's own stem cells are exhausted, life and lifespan naturally end. Each person's supply is a one-time

The body holds many autologous stem cells: the primary life-energy cells - hematopoietic; the secondary - mesenchymal; the tertiary - germline - plus organ stem cells such as neural, pancreatic and retinal

Advantages of BNC-brand autologous stem cells

Technical advantages

Blood-derived production technology uses advanced molecular biology and growth control to process blood mononuclear cells - reversing them within 6-14 days into hundreds of millions of autologous stem cells of a dozen-plus types with directed or multi-lineage potential.

Original patented reverse induction of somatic cells into autologous stem cells - no transgenes, no viral vectors;

Custom preparation of a dozen-plus autologous stem cell types per client, meeting different needs;

Scaled production: 100-200 ml of blood yields hundreds of millions of primary autologous stem cells in 7-11 days;

Product advantages

Stem cell therapy is not any-cell-for-any-disease: different conditions need different cell types, or even combinations.

BNC's products span hematopoietic, male and female germline, neural, retinal, pancreatic, liver, lung, kidney and mesenchymal stem cells - a dozen-plus autologous types.

Personalization advantages

By age, sex, condition and goals, BNC offers customized service - experts tailoring your personal stem cell plan.

The patented blood-derived technology lets anyone - especially adults - produce and use their own stem cells at any stage of life, enabling true application in disease repair, anti-aging care and in-vitro tissue and organ production

Safety of BNC autologous stem cells

All BNC cells are produced to national clinical-grade standards with strict safety testing: sterility, residual analysis, cell-cycle and ploidy, karyotyping, phenotyping, functional identification and nude-mouse tumorigenicity.

What BNC stem cells do

Addressable conditions - treatable or untreatable by conventional means - number over two hundred, including Parkinson's, Alzheimer's, post-stroke sequelae, thyroid disease, coronary disease, hypertension, hyperlipidemia, diabetes, chronic nephritis, cirrhosis, asthmatic bronchitis, lupus, psoriasis, arthritis and degenerative joint disease.

Second: health maintenance and anti-aging.

1. Improves the body's aging genes - aging genes decline while anti-aging, DNA-repair, longevity and tumor-suppressor genes strengthen.

2. Lengthens chromosomal telomeres, extending lifespan and relative youth.

3. Animal (mouse) studies prove long-term regular use extends average lifespan by 30%, up to 4.6 times maximum.

Third: saving money in a bank parallels banking the seeds of life - autologous stem cells. Store your cells young or healthy, so that future illness or injury (trauma, burns, radiation) finds your own cells waiting in the cell bank's liquid nitrogen.

Fourth: autologous stem cells serve as precise biological material for future organ regeneration.
