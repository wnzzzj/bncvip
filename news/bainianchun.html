Hematopoietic Stem Cells Help Blood Lipids Normalize: Real Cases

Published: October 24, 2025

Autologous hematopoietic stem cells help lipid metabolism return to normal - real cases and prevention guide from BNC Guangzhou

Original by BNC Guangzhou, October 23, 2025

BNC Guangzhou

Autologous stem cells

[BNC autologous stem cell case report]

'Arrows all over my check-up - triglycerides five times normal!' Mr. Liang still shudders at the memory. He knew high lipids were a time bomb in his vessels, but lifestyle intervention works slowly for a busy businessman with constant business dinners.

Yet just over a month later, his recheck surprised even the doctors: triglycerides from 9.33 to 3.75, total cholesterol from 5.42 to 2.65, everything else back in range - thanks to an autologous hematopoietic stem cell intervention at BNC Guangzhou.

01

From off the charts to on target: one report's reversal

August 26, 2025 - first visit

The report spelled out the crisis:

- Triglycerides: 9.33 mmol/L (severely elevated);

- Total cholesterol: 5.42 mmol/L (high);

- HDL ('good' cholesterol): low.

Diagnosis: dyslipidemia with high cardiovascular risk.

September 5, 2025 - first intervention

The expert team drew a rigorous plan. After preparing cells from his own blood, Mr. Liang received his first infusion - 219 million autologous hematopoietic stem cells.

October 10, 2025 - results

The recheck alongside the second infusion was inspiring:

Lipids improved greatly - even uric acid returned to normal.

Total cholesterol, LDL and other key markers all reached ideal levels.

This comeback report marked a genuine internal 'metabolic system repair'.

02

The principle: why can autologous stem cells improve lipids?

The core logic of autologous hematopoietic stem cells is repair and restart.

Lipid drugs are outside help processing excess fat in blood; autologous stem cells are a precise internal maintenance crew - drawn from yourself, rejection-free, with potential actions including:

1. Repairing vascular endothelium: damaged endothelium is where lipid deposition begins; stem cells mend it, reducing garbage at the source.

2. Regulating liver metabolism: the liver is the core lipid-processing plant; stem cells improve its function for better handling of triglycerides and cholesterol.

3. Modulating immune inflammation: high lipids often pair with chronic inflammation; immune modulation by stem cells calms the vascular environment and stabilizes lipids.

At the core lies our exclusive patented technology - small-molecule induced autologous stem cell production: from just 75-150 ml of peripheral blood (one tube), it yields hundreds of millions of primary autologous stem cells within 6-12 days, with viability above 99%!

03

Prevention first: the daily art of lowering lipids

Mr. Liang's case opens a new window, yet no technology replaces daily prevention. Everyone is the first guardian of their own lipid health - keep these five practical strategies:

01

Eat well for translucent blood

- Prefer a Mediterranean diet: deep-sea fish, nuts and olive oil (rich in unsaturated fats);

- Embrace fiber: oats, brown rice, beans and leafy greens as intestinal sweepers;

- Firmly 'three lesses': less fried food, less fatty meat, fewer sweets - especially drop sugary drinks and refined pastries.

02

Move more: set the body burning

- Aerobic exercise is key: 150 minutes weekly of brisk walking, jogging or swimming directly lowers triglycerides.

- Add strength training: squats and push-ups twice weekly build muscle and raise basal metabolism.

Control weight: lighten the load

Obesity - especially abdominal - is lipids' breeding ground. Keeping BMI under 24 is among the most direct, effective metabolic improvements.

Limit alcohol: don't overload the liver

Alcohol, especially in quantity, converts directly to triglycerides in the liver; strict limits are among the fastest ways to lower them.

Test regularly: know yourself

Like Mr. Liang, don't fear check-ups. Regular monitoring of lipids, glucose and uric acid catches problems before louder alarms sound.

04

Summary

Health management is moving from symptomatic treatment to root repair. Mr. Liang's experience shows a new possibility - yet however technology advances, healthy habits remain the most cost-effective, reliable medicine.

May we all hold the key of science while practicing daily duty - guarding the vascular health of ourselves and our families.

END
