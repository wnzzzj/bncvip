 A new path: embracing the dawn of regenerative medicine

With conventional treatment offering limited benefit or unbearable side effects, Mr. Lin and his family, after careful deliberation, chose BNC's autologous stem cell repair program.

[Science corner] What is autologous stem cell repair technology?

To help readers understand Mr. Lin's treatment, here is a brief explainer.

03
What are autologous mesenchymal stem cells?

"Autologous" means sourced from oneself. Seed cells are collected from the patient (e.g. blood or fat tissue), then isolated, cultured and expanded in GMP-standard clean laboratories to yield sufficient, vigorous mesenchymal stem cells.

  Mesenchymal stem cells are adult stem cells with two core superpowers: first, powerful immune modulation - like an intelligent pharmacy secreting multiple factors to rebalance the immune system and curb excessive inflammation; second, multi-lineage differentiation and tissue repair.

04
Why autologous?

 The greatest advantage of using one's own cells is zero rejection and very high safety - deploying the body's own regenerative army to precisely treat its own disease, a highly personalized biological strategy.

On this frontier technology, Mr. Lin began his journey of repair.




02

The BNC autologous stem cell repair journey

     On October 20, 2025, the BNC medical team collected Mr. Lin's blood to prepare his dedicated autologous mesenchymal stem cells.


On October 31, 2025, Mr. Lin received his first infusion - 237 million autologous mesenchymal stem cells.
