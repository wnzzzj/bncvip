CGF Hair Regeneration | Awaken Dormant Follicles with Your Own Cells

Published: June 2, 2026

1. A quick transformation: from hair loss to renewed density

See the comparison - the results speak for themselves! In just 1-2 months, thinning parts and receding hairlines show new baby hairs emerging; some follicles grow 1-2 extra hairs, and strands become thicker. Hair loss is distressing, but hope is at hand: BNC's autologous CGF follicle regeneration can restore dense, healthy hair!

2. CGF - the nutritional gold in your own blood

CGF (Concentrated Growth Factors) is third-generation autologous regeneration technology - more active and longer-lasting than PRP.

Simply put: a small draw of your own venous blood is processed with variable-speed centrifugation to concentrate growth factors, platelets and CD34+ stem cells - no external additives, zero allergy, zero rejection.

These components act as a dedicated nutrient serum for follicles - injected directly into the scalp to nourish precisely where it matters, tackling hair loss at the root.

3. Core principle: wake dormant follicles so hair 'grows on its own'

The essence of hair loss is follicle shrinkage, dormancy and malnutrition - strands gradually thin, soften and shed.

CGF attacks the root through three core mechanisms:

1. Activates dormant follicles: 20+ concentrated growth factors directly awaken 'sleeping' follicles, making fine hair thicker and stronger; CD34+ stem cells serve as follicle 'seeds', repairing damaged follicles and regenerating new ones - increasing follicle count at the root.

2. Improves the scalp environment: boosts scalp circulation, controls oil and calms inflammation - resolving greasiness, dandruff and itch to build healthy 'soil' for follicles;

3. Long-lasting nourishment: forms a 3D fibrin scaffold that locks in nutrients and releases them slowly for 14+ days, continuously feeding follicles for a more durable effect.
