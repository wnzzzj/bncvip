Wheals and redness that keep returning, worse with every scratch? BNC's immunity-boosting injection works on the immune root of chronic urticaria, easing recurring itch and lowering flare-up frequency.

Chronic urticaria care - case reports

Case 1: Mr. Zhang, 35, diagnosed with chronic urticaria - frequent widespread flares and unbearable itch. After one course of the immunity program, the leg redness cleared completely and the itching vanished; he returned to normal.

BNC endogenous immunity-boosting injection

Mechanism of action on urticaria

Unlike conventional symptom-suppressing antihistamine use, this program centers on regulating the immune foundation, acting on four levels:

1. Balances disordered immune pathways

Two-way regulation of the Th1/Th2 ratio curbs overactive allergic responses, reduces release of histamine and other itch-mediating inflammation, lowering the odds of sudden wheals and redness at the source while relieving severe itching.

2. Strengthens immune self-control and the hypersensitive constitution

Enhancing regulatory T cells and immune surveillance raises the tolerance threshold, reducing flares triggered by season changes, temperature, diet or stress, and lengthening the interval between relapses.

3. Resolves old lesions and repairs the skin barrier

Improved local microcirculation speeds the fading of thickened crusts, redness and papules, lightens long-standing pigmentation and repairs the damaged epidermis - breaking the itch-scratch-worsen cycle, as seen in the cases' progression from severe widespread lesions back to smooth skin.

4. Reduces drug dependence through periodic long-term care

Rather than suppressing one flare at a time, it gradually rebalances whole-body immunity - suited to chronic urticaria patients with limited benefit from long-term oral drugs, relapse on withdrawal, or established drug dependence.

To break free of chronic urticaria's grip, controlling the skin surface alone is far from enough - the key is addressing the immune imbalance at its root.
