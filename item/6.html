BncAuto®Nestin

Autologous neural stem cells

Code: BNC-ASC07

Product Overview

Autologous neural stem cells reside in the nervous system with the potential to differentiate into neurons, astrocytes and oligodendrocytes - generating abundant brain cell tissue and self-renewing enough to supply large numbers of brain cells. 

They play a key role in nervous-system development, growth and regenerative repair - the source and seed cells of neural regeneration.

Comparable to hereditary neural stem cells, with stable traits and functions

Nerve tissue repair 

Autologous neural stem cells are early neural-development cells with self-renewal and multi-lineage potential - differentiating into specific neural tissue cells to replace declining or damaged neurons, rebuilding neural structure and function.

Life-sustaining power 

They regenerate neural tissue, maintain its normal function, prevent and treat neurological aging and disease - sustaining whole-body vitality.
