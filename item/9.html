BncAuto®CD133

Autologous vascular stem cells

Code: BNC-ASC04

Product Overview

Autologous vascular stem cells reside in blood and vessels with the potential to differentiate into vascular endothelial cells - generating abundant endothelium and self-renewing enough to supply large numbers of endothelial cells. 

They play a key role in vascular development, growth and regenerative repair - the source and seed cells of vascular regeneration.

Comparable to hereditary vascular stem cells, with stable traits and functions

Autologous vascular stem cells - the regenerative energy cells of blood vessels 


Vascular tissue repair 

With self-renewal and multi-lineage potential, they differentiate into vascular endothelial cells - replacing declining or damaged vascular cells, rebuilding vessel structure and improving function. 

Life-sustaining power 

They regenerate vascular tissue, maintain normal function, promote new vessel formation, reduce infarct size and scarring, and improve transport capacity - preventing and treating vascular aging while sustaining whole-body vitality.
