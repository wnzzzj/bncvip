BncAuto®MDR1

Autologous cardiac stem cells

Code: BNC-ASC10

Product Overview

Autologous cardiac stem cells self-renew and differentiate into cardiac lineages - cardiomyocytes, pacemaker cells, transitional cells, endothelial cells, vascular smooth-muscle cells and more. 

They regenerate abundant heart tissue, sustain cardiac renewal, and repair injury, lesions and function (pumping, supply, circulation) - the source and seed cells of cardiac development, growth and repair. 

They secrete multiple factors promoting cardiovascular regeneration, vessel-caliber recovery, greater vessel number and length, and reduced infarct size - a mature high-tech approach with promise against heart disease (atrial fibrillation, coronary disease and more). 

Comparable to hereditary cardiac stem cells, with stable traits and functions
