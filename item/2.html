Autologous mesenchymal stem cells

Product Overview

Autologous mesenchymal stem cells are adult stem cells of the connective-tissue system, capable of long-term self-renewal and differentiation into all connective tissues and cells (skin, fat, bone, joints, ligaments, muscle, cartilage, menisci and other supporting cells).

Comparable to hereditary mesenchymal stem cells, with stable traits and functions

A. Basic characteristics of BncAuto CD29 autologous MSCs

Strong proliferation and multi-lineage differentiation potential 

Under suitable in-vivo or in-vitro conditions, can differentiate into osteoblasts, chondrocytes, adipocytes, myocytes, neural cells, hepatocytes, endothelial cells, stromal cells and more.

Immune modulation 

Through cell-cell interaction and cytokine production, interferes with dendritic-cell function and suppresses T-cell proliferation and immune responses, rebuilding immune balance.


1) Two to three applications of BncAuto CD29 MSCs completely resolved 10 years of severe psoriasis


2) After two courses, 10-year positive anti-endometrial antibodies turned negative and pathological lymphocytes normalized - away from immune infertility


3) Repairs kidney aging and improves immune nephropathy


4) Repairs age-related degenerative knee osteoarthritis


B. Practical application value of BncAuto CD29 MSCs


The soil cells of tissue support

MSCs act as feeder-layer cells present in nearly all tissues, supporting the survival and growth of hematopoietic cells, embryonic stem cells, tumor cells, neural cells, hepatocytes, endothelial cells and more.


The micro-pharmacy of tissue repair

When inflammation releases distress signals, MSCs continuously secrete anti-inflammatory and anti-apoptotic molecules, creating a microenvironment that promotes tissue repair and regeneration.


Signaling switch function

They may possess an innate on/off control keyed to the presence of inflammatory signals.


Anti-inflammatory action

They promote secretion of anti-inflammatory IL-4 and IL-10 while reducing pro-inflammatory IL-12, IL-6 and TNF-alpha - exerting a net anti-inflammatory effect.


Anti-fibrotic action

Can repair fibrosis of blood vessels, liver and lung.


Activating endogenous stem cells

Transplanted MSCs activate endogenous stem cells to carry out tissue repair.


The body's scaffolding

MSCs are the builders and maintainers of tissue: they scaffold tissue morphogenesis, differentiating into bone, cartilage, fat and fibrous connective tissue that frame organ development.


Building the cellular microenvironment

MSCs build and maintain cellular environments: every cell type needs its niche to function; MSCs construct these niches and secrete the factors that sustain them - muscle, neural and other environments.


Tissue function maintainers

MSCs underpin tissue function, and virtually every disease relates to them directly: physical, chemical and biological insults act on the tissue scaffold and cell environments, causing injury, infection, allergy, necrosis and immune disorder.
