The third life-energy cell of women - the source of female life


A. Functions of female autologous germline stem cells 


1) In youth, they are the building material for ovarian development, growth and maturation - the source cells of egg production and ovulation, the architect of development, and the wellspring of youth, joy and health. 

2) In adulthood, they power ovarian function as the mother cells of egg production, sex hormones and sexual function - the ancestral cells of fertility and reproduction. 

3) The exhaustion of ovarian germline stem cells is the essence of ovarian aging - and of female aging itself. They are a woman's third life-energy cell.

Rebuilds and repairs the ovaries, rejuvenating their structure and function;

Holds great potential to extend overall vitality and lifespan;

Long-term maintenance and enhancement of egg production and ovulation;

Long-term maintenance of sexual and reproductive function;

Sustains energy, stamina, endurance and memory;

Systemic anti-aging and health maintenance.
