Application: repair and recovery in sub-health and early-stage disease

When an ECG shows T-wave and ST-segment abnormalities, when brain MRI reveals micro-bleeds or micro-infarcts, when fasting glucose crosses 7.0, when ultrasound shows fatty liver - these sub-health and early-disease states mark the optimal window for autologous stem cell repair and care.
