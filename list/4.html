BNC artificial autologous stem cells
Scientific principle standards

BNC's artificial autologous stem cell production technology uses the physiological effects of small-molecule compounds to recreate, in vitro, the conditions and biological factors for reversing somatic cells into autologous stem cells. Different formulas give different reverse-differentiation capacities; by controlling strength and direction, the technology performs shallow, moderate and deep reverse differentiation on blood-derived mononuclear cells, guiding them into varied degrees of reprogramming and lineage commitment

product standards design

As seed and energy cells for precision repair, anti-aging, emergency and health-insurance medicine, autologous stem cells demand controllable quality, safety and efficacy. What product standards stand behind a premium autologous stem cell product?
BNC's artificial autologous stem cells and their life-science applications are, in essence, high-end biomimetic technology replicating hereditary autologous stem cells. Each product comprehensively emulates a specific natural stem cell's scientific principles, biology and life functions - so that repair, replenishment, energy, immune and reproductive functions are all strongly expressed
From collection of raw cells and isolation of mononuclear cells through preparation to final use, the entire BNC chain follows the national technical guidelines for cell therapy quality control, the guiding principles for stem cell preparation quality and preclinical research, and the ISO 9001 quality system - ensuring activity, stable biological function, stable genetic characteristics and controllable quality

Production environment and process standard design for BNC artificial autologous stem cell products

Stem cells are living preparations - small deviations in production can greatly affect quality, safety and clinical efficacy. Even a previously good research result cannot substitute for strict oversight and verification in batch production, which alone guarantee stable, consistent final quality


The BNC artificial autologous stem cell preparation center is built strictly to GMP requirements - covering site cleanliness, airborne particles, microbial counts (airborne, settling and surface microbes), air velocity, static pressure differential, temperature and relative humidity - to minimize the risks of contamination, cross-contamination, mix-ups and errors during production. to ensure consistently stable products fit for their intended use and registration requirements.Built to CGMP standards beyond GMP-compliant facilities, the BNC center emphasizes staff training and standardized workflows.


The production center includes changing rooms, buffer zones, production suites, culture areas and media preparation rooms, all configured to GMP for air, pressure, microbial counts, lighting and airflow, with operation zones reaching the highest cleanliness classes


Production staff pass qualification exams before starting work all operations follow standard SOPs to ensure quality, safety and sterility. The center is equipped with ESCO biological safety cabinets, CO2 incubators and other internationally advanced equipment for safer, more stable production.

quality testing standard design

As living medicines, autologous stem cells differ markedly from conventional biologics in quality control. They are derived from the patient's own cells and are therefore personalized products.
Under the national guiding principles (trial), BNC products are controlled across four dimensions: Safety, Purity, Potency and Identity.

Identity

BNC artificial autologous stem cells
Product safety testing standard design

BNC uses the client's own somatic cells as raw material to produce stem cells exclusively for that client, in three steps: collection of the client's cells; production and preparation; quality testing.


Production undergoes strict quality and safety testing - cell viability, biological function, tumorigenicity, microbes, mycoplasma and endotoxin. Viability and biological function are crucial to therapeutic effect, while tumorigenicity, microbe, mycoplasma and endotoxin tests are closely tied to safety. Qualified products also undergo release testing before infusion
