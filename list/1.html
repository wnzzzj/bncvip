About BNC
Autologous Stem Cell Regenerative Medicine Group

BNC Stem Cell Regenerative Medicine Technology Group is a national high-tech enterprise with over 20 years in life-energy production technology and precision regenerative medicine R&D and translation. It specializes in non-transgenic autologous stem cell production technology at scale, along with preparation, storage and translational research, holding internationally advanced core technologies and ranking among China's leading autologous stem cell brands.

BNC autologous stem cell overview

Historically, the only source of human autologous stem cells was the hereditary supply gifted by our parents. After 2000, scientists came to see autologous stem cells as each person's precise life-energy cells. In 2007, Prof. Shinya Yamanaka's team solved the world problem of artificially inducing autologous stem cells, earning the 2012 Nobel Prize in Medicine

BNC autologous stem cells
Core production principle

Using the physiological effects of small molecules, the technology recreates in vitro the microenvironments and biological factors for reversing somatic cells into stem cells. Different formulas give different reverse-differentiation strength and direction, performing shallow, moderate and deep reprogramming on blood-derived mononuclear cells

BNC autologous stem cells
Production environment standards

The BNC preparation center produces strictly to GMP requirements - site cleanliness, particle and microbial counts, air velocity, pressure differential, temperature and humidity - minimizing contamination, cross-contamination, mix-ups and errors to ensure consistently stable products fit for their intended use


Built to CGMP standards beyond GMP-compliant facilities and equipment, the BNC center emphasizes staff training and standardized workflows, holding production to the strictest requirements.

BNC autologous stem cells
Product safety testing standards

From collection of raw cells and isolation of mononuclear cells through preparation to final use, the entire BNC chain follows the national technical guidelines for cell therapy quality control, the guiding principles for stem cell preparation quality and preclinical research, and the ISO 9001 quality system - ensuring activity, stable biological function, stable genetic characteristics and controllable quality

BNC Chief Scientist - Dr. Lin Xiongbin

Senior expert in autologous stem cell technology and regenerative medicine
Life scientist and thinker of autologous stem cells

1998: Became the first Chinese scientist granted German citizenship on the recommendation of the German Ministry of the Interior for achievements in autologous stem cell research.


2002: First ethnic-Chinese scientist to publish a stem cell research paper in Nature.
The same year, founded the Nanning Regenerative Cell Transplant Center - the first in Guangxi and among the earliest in China.


2004: Founded China's first specialist stem cell transplant hospital - Guilin Shengzhiyuan Stem Cell Transplant Hospital.


2019: Summarizing 20+ years of R&D and translation, published China's first monograph on autologous stem cells - 'New Advances in Life Science: The Breakthrough of Blood-Derived Autologous Stem Cells and Their Production Technology'.
